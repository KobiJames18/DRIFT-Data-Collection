Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

DRI-FT ("we", "us") runs online registration, physical ticket activation, and check in for our events. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

What we collect

Depending on how you register or activate a ticket, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • A photo of you, if you activate a physical ticket (used only to help door staff confirm your identity at check in)
  • Any other details our registration or activation form asks for at the time of your event

About physical ticket PINs

If you buy a physical ticket, it comes with a hidden PIN that you enter online to activate it. We never store your PIN as readable text, only a one way security hash. Nobody, including our own staff, can look up your PIN after it's created.

Why we collect it

We use this information to:

  • Confirm your registration or ticket and manage attendee lists
  • Contact you with event related updates (schedule changes, entry instructions, and so on)
  • Let door staff confirm your identity visually against your photo when checking you in

What we don't do

We do not sell your information to anyone. We do not share it with third parties except where strictly necessary to run the event (for example, a venue's security checking a registration list at the door).

Who can see your data

Only authorized DRI-FT admins can access registration data. Door staff (scanner accounts) can only look up one ticket at a time to check someone in; they cannot browse the full list of registrants. Access is restricted and logged on our end.

How long we keep it

We retain registration and ticket data for as long as reasonably needed for the event and basic record keeping afterward. You can request deletion at any time (see below).

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what data we hold on you, correct it, or delete it, including your photo. Contact us using the details below and we'll handle it promptly.
