Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

DRI-FT ("we", "us") runs event registration for our events. This page explains what information we collect when you register, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

What we collect

When you register for an event, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Any other details our registration form asks for at the time of your event

Why we collect it

We use this information to:

  • Confirm your registration and manage attendee lists
  • Contact you with event related updates (schedule changes, entry instructions, etc.)
  • Verify eligibility for age-restricted events

What we don't do

We do not sell your information to anyone. We do not share it with third parties except where strictly necessary to run the event (for example, a venue's security checking a registration list at the door).

Who can see your data

Only authorized DRI-FT admins can access registration data. Access is restricted and logged on our end.

How long we keep it

We retain registration data for as long as reasonably needed for the event and basic record-keeping afterward. You can request deletion at any time (see below).

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what data we hold on you, correct it, or delete it. Contact us using the details below and we'll handle it promptly.
