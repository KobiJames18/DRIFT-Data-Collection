Frequently Asked Questions

Is DRIFT really 18+?

Yes. All DRIFT events require attendees to be 18 or older. Be ready to confirm your age at entry.

How do I register?

Fill out the registration form on our homepage with your name, phone number, and email. You'll get a confirmation once it's submitted.

Will I get a confirmation email or message?

You'll see an on-screen confirmation with a reference ID right after you submit. There's no automatic email or SMS yet — once your registration is reviewed and approved, our team will reach out to you directly by phone or email.

Can I register on someone else's behalf?

No. Each person must register with their own name, phone number, and email. Registering on behalf of someone else isn't allowed.

What happens to my personal information?

Check our Privacy Policy for full details on what we collect and why.
