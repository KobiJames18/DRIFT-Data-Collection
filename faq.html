Frequently Asked Questions

General

Is DRI-FT really 18+?

Yes. All DRI-FT events require attendees to be 18 or older. Be ready to confirm your age at entry.

Online registration

How do I register?

Fill out the registration form on our homepage with your name, phone number, and email. You'll get an on screen confirmation with a QR coded reference ID once it's submitted.

Will I get a confirmation email or message?

You'll see an on screen confirmation right after you submit. There's no automatic email or SMS yet, once your registration is reviewed and approved, our team will reach out to you directly by phone or email.

Can I register on someone else's behalf?

No. Each person must register with their own name, phone number, and email. Registering on behalf of someone else isn't allowed.

Physical tickets

I bought a physical ticket. What do I do with it?

Go to our Activate Ticket page, enter the ticket code printed on it, then enter the hidden PIN (scratch it off if it's covered). You'll then fill in your name, phone, email, and a photo of yourself to complete activation.

What's the PIN for?

It's a security step that only the real ticket holder can complete, since the PIN is hidden until you have the physical ticket in hand. This stops someone from registering a ticket using just a photo of the QR code.

Why do you need a photo?

Door staff use it to visually confirm you're the same person who activated the ticket when you check in. It's only ever shown to staff during check in.

My ticket code shows "already registered". What happened?

Each physical ticket can only be activated once. If you're sure you haven't activated it yourself, contact us using the details below, someone else may have gotten hold of your ticket code and PIN.

At the door

What do I need to bring to get in?

Your ticket code or QR code (physical ticket or the one from your online registration), and be ready to show ID confirming you're 18 or older.
